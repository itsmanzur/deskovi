What's included

Everything a WooCommerce store needs, in the free version

No trial, no "upgrade to unlock." These ship in Deskovi core.

Order-aware tickets

Every ticket auto-links to the WooCommerce order it's about — no manual lookup.

Guest ticket access

Customers verify with order number + billing email. No account, no extra password.

Secure attachments

Uploads are locked outside direct access, served through signed, ownership-checked links.

Agent roles & assignment

A dedicated agent role with just enough capability — assign tickets by name, free.

Canned replies
