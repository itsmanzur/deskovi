Why teams switch

Three things most helpdesk plugins won't tell you

We used to run Deskovi through a hosted backend too — then we removed it. Here's what that decision changes for you.

Zero external account, ever

No SaaS signup, no vendor lock-in, no data quietly leaving your server. Every ticket, message and attachment lives in your own WordPress database — nowhere else.

Self-contained by design

Guest-friendly by default

Customers get help with their order number and billing email — verified by a one-time code, no WordPress account required. Nobody creates yet another password to ask for help.

No forced signups

A free core with real depth

Assignment, canned replies, priority, bulk actions, CSV export and refunds from the ticket all ship in the free core. Pro is for extras like reply-by-email — not for the basics.
