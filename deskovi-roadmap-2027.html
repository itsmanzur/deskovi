What the research says

Read from each vendor's own pricing, feature and WordPress.org pages on 16 Nov 2026. "Not found" means the page did not say it, not that the product lacks it.

  • Customers expect an AI answer before a ticket. SupportCandy gives an AI chatbot in its free plugin; JS Help Desk, Support Genix, ThriveDesk and Gorgias sell one. Deskovi has agent-side AI and a self-service panel, but nothing that answers the customer. This is the largest gap. → Pro 1.2
  • Order actions are where Deskovi leads. Every WordPress helpdesk checked shows order details; none of their pages mention refunding, cancelling or editing an order from the ticket. Deskovi does all of it. Returns and tracking extend that lead. → Pro 1.3
  • Other free plugins give more reach. Fluent Support ships a customer portal, REST API and importer free; SupportCandy ships 15 custom field types free. Deskovi Free has no My Account page or order "Get help" button yet. → Free 1.6
  • Fluent Support ships every 4 to 8 weeks. Six feature releases between April and November 2026 (agent groups, MCP server, multi-provider AI, advanced reports, remote portal, scheduled replies). A steady cadence matters as much as any one feature.
  • Lifetime and first-year deals are normal here. SupportCandy and Support Genix sell lifetime licenses; Fluent Support and Heroic Inbox discount the first year by 40 to 50%. Deskovi is annual only.
  • Price position is fine. $79 for one site matches SupportCandy and undercuts Fluent Support ($129), JS Help Desk ($99) and Heroic Inbox ($199). No reason to cut price; the work is features and proof.
